# Two Sum

**LeetCode Problem:** 1  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Topic:** Arrays & Strings

## Problem
Find two numbers whose sum equals the target.

## Approach
1. Use two loops to check pairs of numbers.
2. Add the two numbers.
3. If their sum equals the target, return their indices.

## Time Complexity
O(n²)

## Space Complexity
O(1)

Accepted on LeetCode.
